Examination & certification ecosystem

Connecting Learning with
Recognised Assessment Pathways.

Performing arts education means more when students can measure progress and validate their skills through credible assessment. Through Ikigai Schools, Airavut connects learning with established Indian and international examination and certification pathways.

Ikigai Schools branches serve as examination centres within an ecosystem that includes:

Trinity College London

International graded assessment and certification pathways across selected music and performing arts disciplines.

Rockschool / RSL Awards, London

Contemporary music assessment and graded certification pathways, built around progressive musicianship and practical skills.

Prayag Sangeet Samiti

Established Indian examination and certification pathways across Indian classical music and dance.
