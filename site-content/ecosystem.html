Our verticals

Three Verticals. One Education Platform.

Ikigai Schools

Learning, Training & Skill Development

Airavut’s physical learning platform: multi-disciplinary performing arts schools with structured training in music, vocals, dance and theatre, across Indian and Western disciplines.

Ikigai Schools also creates careers for artists as teachers, academic leaders, curriculum contributors and education professionals.

Explore careers at Ikigai Schools

Ikigai Exams

Exams, Grading, Certification & Employment Readiness

Airavut’s assessment and certification vertical, being developed to provide structured frameworks for skill assessment, grading, certification and employment readiness across performing arts disciplines.

Built for Ikigai Schools students and external learners, so the exam ecosystem scales beyond Airavut’s own schools.

Explore Ikigai Exams

Ikigai Publishing

Learning Methodology & Exam Books

The intellectual-property and content engine of Airavut. It turns Airavut’s methodology, curriculum and assessment frameworks into course books, exam books and resources for students, educators and institutions.

Publishing takes the Airavut methodology beyond the walls of an Ikigai School.
