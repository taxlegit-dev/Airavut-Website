Meet the founder

Shweta Singh

Founder & Director

Building an Education Pathway for Performing Arts.

Shweta Singh is the founder and driving force behind Airavut Education Ventures. After more than 16 years of corporate experience in sales, marketing and business development, and around a decade running a music and dance education franchise, she founded Ikigai Schools in 2025.

Her journey from running a performing arts school to building an integrated education company shaped a larger vision:

Airavut was created to turn that vision into a structured ecosystem of learning, skill development, examinations, certification, publishing and employment opportunities for artists.

Her long-term ambition: an Indian-origin, world-class performing arts education platform with global relevance and scale.
