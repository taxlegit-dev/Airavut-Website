Assessment vertical

Measuring Progress. Validating Skill

Performing arts education means more when students can measure progress and validate their skills through credible assessment.

Ikigai Exams & Certifications is Airavut’s developing assessment vertical. It measures practical proficiency, progression and readiness, and is designed for Ikigai Schools students and external learners.
